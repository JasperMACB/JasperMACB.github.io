Look at My Awesome Bike!

I found this bike abandoned in the storage room of my old apartment building. It's a Fuji S-12S from 1981, a mid range road bike, as identified from the serial number. After some inspection and test riding, I determined the fork was damaged from a previous crash, so I bought a new one on eBay and replaced it. Additionally, I gave it some new brakes and a good cleaning. Since then, it's back to its previous glory, and I've ridden it all around the Seattle area. Isn't she a beauty?
